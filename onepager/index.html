The Problem

Template packs tell you what the document is.
Not when you need it, or what to do with it.

◇ What exists
Folders of empty templates. Certification material that lists 49 processes as if every project needs all of them. Dashboards full of numbers like CPI and SPI with no explanation of what to do when they slip. Most of it is written for people who already know the jargon.
✦ What the toolkit does
Starts from your project, not from the template list. Shows only the documents that kind of project needs, in the order you'll need them. Explains every document and every metric in plain English, so a new PM, a sponsor or a client can follow along too.
The Core Mechanic

Two ways in, both ending in an answer.

Every path through the toolkit ends in something you can act on. Start from your project and you end up with when, how, who and what to update. Start from a number on your status report and you end up with its impact and the steps to fix it.
