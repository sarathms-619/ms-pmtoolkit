Your CPI is 0.90. Now what?

I've sat in review meetings where that number came up. Everyone nodded. Someone said "slightly over budget". Next slide.

Here's what it was actually saying: every ₹1 we spent was buying 90 paise of work. On a ₹2 crore project, that's about ₹22 lakh over by the end. And after the first few months, it rarely fixes itself.

The fix isn't complicated:
→ Find the one workstream burning the money
→ Check what the vendor contract says about sharing the overrun
→ Trim scope before you trim quality
→ Two bad months in a row? Tell your sponsor now, not after the third
