About

The career nobody planned.

I didn't set out to be a resource manager. I set out to make things work — and resource management turned out to be the thing that was most broken, most invisible, and most consequential in every environment I walked into.

14 years across four very different organisations — an IT services firm, Big 4, elite management consulting, and a lean design studio — and the pattern that emerged wasn't planned. It was revealed. Every role I've held has had one thing in common: the information leadership needed to make decisions either didn't exist or wasn't being surfaced. I built the systems that surfaced it.

At BCG I owned the Middle East DRS function and five India practice areas simultaneously, overseeing 15 people globally. At EY I was the single point of contact for Risk Transformation staffing across EMEIA, APAC, and Oceania. In both cases, my title understated the scope by a significant margin — a pattern I've since learned is structural, not accidental, in consulting environments.

More recently I've started building the tools I wish had existed — AI-powered operational infrastructure that reduces manual effort in delivery, resourcing, hiring, and reporting. 20+ tools, all live, without a developer background. That last part matters: the constraint is what sharpened the thinking.

Outside work: I'm working towards my PMP. I watch too much anime and build things nobody asked me to build. Talk to me about anime, football , cricket, and food — great conversations happen over a chai .

Recruitment Intelligence Portal
End-to-end AI hiring infrastructure — from first resume to final decision — built for a 50+ person design studio with no dedicated TA team.
① Problem
NetBramha had no structured hiring process. Resumes arrived over email, screening was manual and inconsistent, pipeline visibility was zero, and hiring decisions were made on gut. With 10–15 open roles at any point, the process was costing weeks per hire.
② Solution
A unified recruitment portal combining two AI tools: First Cut (AI resume screener with shortlist verdicts in ~30 seconds) and Hiring Intelligence (live candidate pipeline with AI forecasting, bottleneck diagnosis, and talent pool reuse). One portal. One source of truth.

Hiring Intelligence tracks candidates across stages with claim locking for multi-user workflows. No code dependencies, no external SaaS costs.
④ My Value Add
Identified the gap, scoped the system, built both tools end-to-end without developer support. Cut initial candidate assessment from 8 to 2 minutes, and screening time per batch from 3 hours to under 30 minutes. Gave the studio its first real view of hiring pipeline health — roles, stages, conversion, and bottlenecks — in one dashboard.

The Mettle
"Two ways to do this job. The textbook way. And the real way. This is the real way."
① Problem
Every PM framework teaches what to do in ideal conditions. Nobody teaches what to do when the organisation pushes back — when the process on paper exists to protect leadership rather than delivery. The gap between frameworks and organisational reality is where most PMs struggle, alone, without language.
② Solution
A five-module PM operating system: an environment diagnostic that builds your actual operating model, a situation-to-law matcher across 19 principles, seven survival scenarios from real delivery experience, a toolkit builder with 22 exportable templates, and a consequence-based branching simulation engine.
③ Approach
Built from 14 years of field experience across BCG, EY, and NetBramha — not from textbooks. Every scenario, every law, every template is drawn from real situations. The AI layer personalises each response to the visitor's exact wording and context. No generic answers.
